# Taxation and Benefits Module - Backend Documentation

## Overview

Tax calculations, exemptions, proofs, and flexible benefits management.

## Core Doctypes

### Income Tax Slab
- Progressive tax brackets
- Annual tax calculation
- Monthly TDS deduction

### Employee Tax Exemption Declaration
- Annual exemption declarations
- HRA, 80C, 80D exemptions
- Proof submission requirements

### Employee Benefit Application
- Flexi-benefit claims
- Max benefit limits
- Proof-based approvals

## Key Rules
- Tax calculated on annual projected income
- HRA exemption based on rent paid
- Benefits allocated within limits
- Proofs required for exemptions
